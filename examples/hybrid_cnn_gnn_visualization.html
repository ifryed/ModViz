How to use this diagram

This is a self-contained ModViz network-flow view of a traced PyTorch model. Rearrange freely; exports use your current layout.

Navigate

  • Drag empty canvas to pan.
  • Mouse wheel zooms toward the pointer. Shift+wheel or a horizontal wheel pans sideways.
  • Fit frames the whole figure. + / − zoom in steps.
  • Search dims layers whose label or target does not match.

Select and move

  • Click a layer to select it and open details on the right.
  • Shift-click adds or removes layers or stage panels from the selection.
  • Shift-drag on empty canvas draws a marquee; an object is selected only when its entire box is inside.
  • Drag a layer, or a stage panel’s empty area / title, to move the current selection. Connectors reroute while you drag.

Groups and alignment

  • With two or more objects selected, floating tools appear: Align H, Align V, and Group.
  • When a group is selected: rename (pencil), Ungroup, or Minimize / Expand.
  • Minimized groups hide internal layers; external edges reattach to the compact cell.

Toolbar

  • Losses toggles loss operations, their connections, and loss-only groups.
  • Reset restores the automatic layout.
  • Undo or Ctrl/Cmd+Z reverts layout edits (up to 50 steps).
  • PNG, SVG, PDF, and draw.io export the full diagram, not only the visible viewport.
